# Roadmap

*Version française : [ROADMAP.fr.md](ROADMAP.fr.md)*

These are **intentions, without dates**. Each version comes when it is ready and tested; the order and the content
can still change. Your ideas are welcome: use **Suggest an improvement** in Prévo (About screen or foot of the menu), or
the [issues](https://github.com/prevo-pro/prevo/issues).

## 2.0 — installation, two languages, safer data

- An **installation assistant**, accounts and access from the phone.
- **French and English**: the whole interface, the help and the server; each person can keep their own language.
- A **versioned data format** with automatic migration, **automatic backups**, and a complete **ZIP export and
  import**.
- An **update notice** (one anonymous read of a public file per day, which can be switched off).
- A **Suggest an improvement** button.
- Drafts kept on the device for a breakdown report and a job report.

## 2.1 — more languages, then the trainer mode

- **Turkish, Italian and Portuguese** (Portugal), in machine translation at first: the interface says so, and
  corrections are welcome (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). Done for version 2.1.0.
- **Trainer mode**: packs of breakdown scenarios distributed as ZIP files, one copy per trainee, reset in one click.

## 2.2 — "the manager sleeps well"

An aid to follow-up; **the responsibility stays with the employer**, and the regulatory intervals will be sourced and
editable.

- Periodic regulatory inspections, with follow-up of the **reservations** of each inspection report, one by one.
- Authorisations and CACES (French plant operator certificates) with their **refresher dates**.
- **Warranties** and **service contracts**.
- The **cost of downtime** in the monthly report.

## 2.3 — workshop

Prévo already has a parts store and hour counters; 2.3 builds on them.

- The equipment record **filled in by the AI from a photo of the rating plate**.
- **Spare parts** with minimum stock and links to the machines.
- **Hour counters** for usage-based preventive maintenance.
- A **lockout sheet per machine**, opened by QR code: an aid, which **does not replace the lockout procedure**.

## 2.4 — hosted mode

- An **app you can install on the phone** (PWA).
- **Notifications of urgent breakdowns** (e-mail or ntfy).

## Ongoing — a community library

A **library of preventive maintenance plans by machine type**, importable into Prévo and enriched by the community
on GitHub.
